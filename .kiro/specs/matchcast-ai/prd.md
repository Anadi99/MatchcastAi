# Product Requirements Document (PRD)
# MatchCast AI — World Cup 2026

## 1. Product Vision

MatchCast AI brings live football commentary to Indian fans in their own language. Instead of watching a match on TV, fans subscribe once on Telegram and receive punchy, emotional, real-commentator-style updates in Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, or Marathi — every 60 seconds and on every key moment. The goal is to make the World Cup feel personal and local for 100M+ Indian fans who currently consume cricket commentary in their mother tongue but have no equivalent for football.

Revenue target: ₹12L over the 6-week World Cup 2026 window.
Build timeline: 14 days, solo developer.

---

## 2. Problem Statement

Indian football fans have no native-language commentary experience for live international football. English broadcasts are generic. Hindi TV commentary exists but is not accessible on-demand or on mobile messaging platforms. There is no product that delivers hyper-local, language-specific, AI-powered football commentary directly to a user's phone without requiring a separate app install.

## 4. Core Features

### 4.1 Language-Native Commentary
- Hindi (dramatic, high-emotion), Tamil (poetic, lyrical), Telugu (energetic, exclamatory), Marathi (conversational local tone)
- All generated by Gemini 1.5 Flash with sport-specific, language-specific prompts
- Free tier: Hindi only. Premium tier: all four languages.

### 4.2 Live Event Push
- Every goal, card, substitution, and VAR decision triggers an instant push within 10 seconds
- Commentary is 2–3 sentences, max 60 words, ends with a reaction line

### 4.3 60-Second Pulse
- Even in quiet spells, a match situation update fires every 60 seconds
- Includes possession, tempo, tactical observation

### 4.4 Match Summary
- At full time, a 5-line shareable report: score, key moments, player of match, tactical verdict, emotional close
- Delivered to all match subscribers; formatted for easy Telegram/WhatsApp forwarding

### 4.5 Telegram Bot
- Primary interface: /start, /language, /matches, /subscribe, /unsubscribe, /status, /premium, /help
- Inline keyboard for language selection and match subscription

### 4.6 Web Feed (matchcast.in)
- No install required; shows live commentary feed in browser
- Language toggle, match selector, mobile-first layout
- Sponsor messages embedded at 4th-update cadence

### 4.7 Sponsor Injection
- Every 4th update to free users carries a sponsor message
- Sponsors pay per week (₹50K–₹2L), not per click
- Suppressed for premium users

### 4.8 Premium Subscription
- ₹99/month via Razorpay
- Unlocks: all 4 languages, no ads, pre-match + post-match reports

### 4.9 White-Label API (post-WC)
- REST API for third-party publishers
- ₹5K–₹20K/month; API key auth, 60 req/min rate limit

## 7. Out of Scope (v1)

- WhatsApp delivery (Twilio — stretch goal, not in 14-day build)
- Leagues beyond FIFA World Cup 2026
- Video clips or GIFs
- In-app purchases beyond Razorpay subscription
- iOS/Android native app

---

## 8. Constraints

- API-Football free tier: 100 requests/day cap — polling strategy must be conservative outside live windows
- Gemini 1.5 Flash: rate limits apply; implement retry + fallback
- Build: 14 days, 1 developer
- Hosting budget: Railway.app free/starter tier
